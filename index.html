About

Building with
intention.

I live where design meets execution, fluent in Figma, Illustrator, and the creative process end-to-end. As Design Director at Redstone Studios, I lead projects from concept through commission, shaping brands and digital products that hold up under scrutiny.

Before that, I founded Atlas Minigames, turning a $50 startup budget into over $20K in revenue while building design systems that improved brand consistency by 60%. I believe the best work comes from understanding both the vision and the community behind it.

Outside of client work, I'm hands-on with IT infrastructure, self-hosting services on Raspberry Pi clusters and managing home lab environments. I approach technology the same way I approach design: understand the system first, then make it better. Whether it's configuring networks, automating deployments, or building brand systems from scratch, I care about the details that compound over time.
