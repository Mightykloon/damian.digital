About

Hardware, software, and art
this is me :)

Researching for a safer, secure, and sustainable future.

I build hardware, write software, and make art. Because if you aren't making something, what the hell are you doing?!

Right now most of my time goes into Nullberry, prototyping the MK-I: an off-grid, jam-proof messenger with a 9-layer fallback protocol, built on a fork of Reticulum. It's made for disasters, protests, and places without reliable infrastructure, and every unit out there makes the network stronger.

I'm also Design Director at Redstone Studios, a Minecraft creative studio, and I keep chipping away at re:mixd, a place to share and edit playlists with friends across Spotify, Apple Music, and Tidal. Before all that I founded Atlas Minigames and turned $50 into $20K+ in its first three months.

I study Computer Network Systems Engineering at Moorpark College, and at home I torture myself with a homelab, self-hosting on old hardware and redesigning stuff that already worked fine.
