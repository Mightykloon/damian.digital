01 About

Research for a safer, secure, and sustainable future.

I build hardware, write software, and make art. The best ideas happen where disciplines overlap.

At Nullberry I serve as Lead R&D Officer, designing prototypes for a wireless and wired nine-layer fallback protocol communication device. It is built for mass organized protests, natural disasters, and third-world countries at scalable cost, unreliant on any existing infrastructure except its own growing system.

I'm also building re:mixd, a unique social media platform for sharing music and playlists across streaming services that don't usually connect well with each other, so what you listen to can travel with you and your friends.

As Design Director at Redstone Studios I lead projects from concept through commission, shaping brands and digital products that hold up under scrutiny. Before that, I founded Atlas Minigames and turned a $50 startup budget into over $20K in revenue. These days I'm studying Computer Network Systems Engineering, learning the wires underneath the internet.

Off the clock you'll find me self-hosting services on Raspberry Pi clusters, 3D-printing prototypes, or redesigning something that was working perfectly fine.
